Awula is a standalone single, released March 5, 2026, the day before Ghana marked its 69th Independence Day, and submitted on its own for Best African Music Performance.

Built around Obofour Raphael's lead vocal, production and arrangement, Awula sits close to the ground rhythmically, with guitar, violin and background vocals filled in by a small, dedicated cast.

It is not part of the Adom Akyedeɛ album; it stands as its own release, submitted alongside Obofour Raphael's other 2027 GRAMMY® entries — Adom Akyedeɛ and Ahoofe — as part of the same campaign.
