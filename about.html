About Obofour Raphael

Silvanus Raphael Oduro Dickson, popularly known as Obofour Raphael, is a Ghanaian Highlife/Hiplife artist whose music blends traditional Ghanaian rhythms with contemporary sounds, delivering powerful messages of faith, hope, and redemption.

Born on April 10th in Brescia, in the Lombardy region of Italy, Obofour Raphael's journey began far from Ghana, but his heart has always been rooted in his Ghanaian heritage. He was raised in Dansoman, Accra, where his passion for music was nurtured alongside his educational journey.

His educational path took him through Ave Maria School for his primary education, followed by Opoku Ware Secondary School. Currently, he is pursuing a degree in Computer Science at the University of Staffordshire in the United Kingdom, demonstrating his commitment to both music and education.

From a young age, Obofour discovered his talent for rap and actively participated in rap battles within his community. In 2003, he formed his first music group, Akasafour (which translates to "Communicators"), alongside his elder brother. Together, they recorded their first demo tape, marking the beginning of his professional music journey.

After the group parted ways when his brother stepped away from music, Obofour chose to continue his solo career, driven by his unwavering passion for the craft. His journey represents resilience, faith, and dedication to using music as a vehicle for positive change.

Career Timeline

2003

Formation of Akasafour

Formed music group Akasafour (Communicators) with elder brother. Recorded and released first demo tape, marking the beginning of professional music journey.

2005-2010

Rise to Prominence

Released several popular tracks enjoyed significant airplay including "Konkonsa (Telephone No Wire)" featuring Scooby Selah, "Kyakya", "Megye Me Sika", and "Gye Wo Kawa".

2015-2020

International Collaboration

Worked with Sadat X from the famous American Hip Hop Group Brand Nubian, Thomas Woernle, a renowned violinist from Germany and Craig David for interpolating "Booty Man" after clearance from Warner Chappel Music and Round Hill Publishing.

2015-2020

Associate Productions

Released iconic singles "Obaa", "Awula", "Megye Me Sika", "Ahuofe" featuring Ko-Jo Cue alongside the legendary vocalist, Nana Ama and "Yesu" featuring Morris Babyface. Collaborated with renowned producers including Zapp Mallet, Kwik Action, Roro, Kin Dee and Fortune Dane.

2024

Adom Akyedee Album

Released groundbreaking album "Adom Akyedee" featuring 18 tracks. Made history by securing clearance from the British superstar Craig David to sample elements from his hit tracks.

Present

Continuing the Legacy

Currently pursuing Computer Science degree at University of Staffordshire while continuing to create music that inspires faith and hope across generations.
